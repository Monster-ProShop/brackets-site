Buy-ins and payouts

Bracket first- and second-place prizes use fixed amounts. High Game and Doubles use the percentage-based prize rules shown below. Buy-ins are per bracket, High Game entry, or bowler per Doubles team.

Prize rounding is in $50 MXN increments for peso events or $5 USD increments for dollar events. Currency changes are locked once registration commitments or results exist.

Handicap High Game Pot

90% of total High Game entries is divided into three equal game pools. Awards round DOWN to the competition's currency increment. The admin retains at least 10% plus rounding remainders.

Doubles — Series Only

Only the combined three-game series (with both bowlers' handicaps) is eligible for a prize. No individual-game prizes are paid.

Enter the percentage of the TOTAL Doubles purse for every paid place (for example: 35%, 20%, 15%, 10%, 5%). The percentages may add to less than 100%, but not more. The remainder is retained by the admin.

Each winning team prize rounds down in the competition's currency before splitting between partners.

Brackets

The draw creates the maximum number of complete eight-bowler brackets within each bowler's limit, while minimizing repeated bracket groups and first-round opponents. Fewer than eight entrants cannot form a bracket. Unused willingness is not charged.

A final-game tie is decided by handicap series, then highest handicap game. If both remain tied, the combined first and second prizes are split equally.
